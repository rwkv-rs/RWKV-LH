$n$ people are seated in a circle around a table, numbered from $1$ to $n$. You can only swap the positions of two adjacent people at a time. Determine the number of swaps required to reverse the order: each person's left-hand neighbor should move to their right and their right-hand neighbor should move to their left. In other words, the orientation of the seating order should change from clockwise to counterclockwise.

The input will be given via stdin and the output should be printed to stdout by your code.

Now solve the problem by providing the code.
