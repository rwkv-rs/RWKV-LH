In the universe, there are $n$ planets. Your task is to transport $k$ supercomputers from planet $S$ to planet $T$ in the shortest possible time. Each supercomputer requires an entire spaceship for transportation. There are $m$ bidirectional tunnels between the planets, and each tunnel takes one day to traverse. No two spaceships may use the same tunnel simultaneously. Tunnels do not connect the same planet to itself, and there is at most one tunnel between any pair of planets.

The input will be given via stdin and the output should be printed to stdout by your code.

Now solve the problem by providing the code.
