Given a date that includes the year, month, and day, guaranteed to be between January 1, 1900, and November 4, 2001. Two players take turns performing one of the following actions:

1. Move to the next day.
2. Move to the same day in the next month (if that day does not exist, this action cannot be chosen).

The player who makes the move that lands exactly on November 4, 2001, wins the game. The question is whether the first player has a winning strategy.

The input will be given via stdin and the output should be printed to stdout by your code.

Now solve the problem by providing the code.
