The Nubian Queen Sultan has no children, so she plans to divide her kingdom into at most k parts before she passes away. The region that performs best in the upcoming test will inherit her estate (and might even inherit it all). To ensure that the person left standing is the smartest, the queen has devised a clever test. In a hall filled with fountains, there are k 8x8 chessboards, each embellished with diamond-studded queens. Each square on the chessboard has a score, and the test-taker must place eight queens on the board such that they do not attack each other, finding a configuration where the sum of the scores of the squares occupied by the queens is maximized. (The queens are chess queens in this problem.)

# Input:

Input starts with an integer k, indicating there are k chessboards. This is followed by k 8x8 matrices, each describing a chessboard.

# Output:

Output the highest possible score attainable.

```

### Example Output #1

260
```
