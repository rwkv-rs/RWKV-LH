The Brazilian Football League is Brazil's most significant competition. In this event, football teams participate, each playing two matches (home and away). A team earns three points for a win, one point for a draw. No points are awarded for a loss.

After the matches conclude, teams are ranked from 1 to n based on their total score. A participating team with a score of p is ranked as 1 plus the number of teams with more than p points. There may be multiple teams with the same rank.

In addition to the champion (the team ranked first), if applicable, the WonderTeam also wins. A team with the absolute highest number of wins (absolute meaning no other team has the same number of wins), absolute highest number of goals scored, and absolute lowest number of goals conceded is known as the WonderTeam. (The WonderTeam must possess all these attributes.)

Your task is to determine the worst possible ranking of the WonderTeam.

The input will be given via stdin and the output should be printed to stdout by your code.

Now solve the problem by providing the code.
